# 🦷 AI-Assisted Dental Clinic Management System

An AI-Assisted desktop application designed to help dental clinics digitize patient records, streamline appointment management, and reduce missed appointments through predictive analytics and automated reminders.

---

## Tech Stack

- **Frontend:** Electron
- **Backend:** Node.js (Express)
- **Database:** MySQL / PostgreSQL
- **Artificial Intelligence:** Llama
- **Cloud Platform:** Google Cloud
- **Notifications:** SMTP Email

---

## Core Features

- **Digital Appointment Management:** Schedule, reschedule, and cancel appointments with real-time conflict detection to prevent double bookings.

- **Patient Record Management:** Store and manage digital patient records, including personal information, medical history, dental history, and treatment notes.

- **AI No-Show Prediction:** Analyze appointment history to identify patients at high risk of missing appointments and proactively assist clinic staff.

- **Automated Appointment Reminders:** Send scheduled email reminders to patients and notify staff when follow-up actions are needed.

- **Vacant Slot Reassignment:** Detect cancelled appointments and recommend available patients from the waiting list to maximize clinic productivity.

- **AI Assistant:** A Llama-powered chatbot capable of summarizing patient records, generating clinic reports, and assisting staff with information retrieval.

- **Role-Based Access Control:** Separate system access for dentists, secretaries, and patients to ensure secure handling of sensitive information.

- **Digital Treatment Notes:** Allow dentists to record diagnoses, procedures, and follow-up recommendations directly within each patient's record.

- **Reporting Dashboard:** Generate appointment summaries, patient statistics, and clinic performance reports.

- **Cloud-Ready Architecture:** Designed for deployment using cloud-hosted databases and services for secure data storage and accessibility.

---

## Project Goal

The system aims to replace traditional paper-based record management with an intelligent digital platform that improves clinic efficiency, reduces administrative workload, and enhances patient care through AI-assisted automation.
